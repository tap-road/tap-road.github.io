Wave Rider is an endless surfing game with a bright summer look. You ride a surfboard or a jet ski across the waves and try to go as far as you can. Logs, rocks, holes and waterfalls block the way. Your board picks up speed over time, so your timing has to keep up. Starfish unlock dozens of new boards and jet skis!

How to Play Wave Rider Unblocked?

  • Moving: Use the arrow keys or the W, A and D keys to steer.
  • Dodging: Stay clear of every obstacle, because one hit ends the run and there is no revive.
  • Starfish: Pick them up to buy new gear, but don't risk a run for just a few.
