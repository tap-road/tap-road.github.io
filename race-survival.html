Race Survival is a 2D car survival game set in a hexagon-shaped arena. The floor is made of tiles, and any tile you stay on too long drops away. Your task is to keep driving and stay on the arena while it breaks apart. Rival cars share the same floor, so you can push or block them off the edge. Hold on until the end and the win is yours!

How to Play Race Survival Unblocked?

  • Steering: Press A or the Left arrow key to turn left, and D or the Right arrow key to turn right.
  • Jumping: Press W or Space to jump.
  • Rewards: Pick up coins, keys and stars, then spend them on new cars.
  • Staying alive: Keep moving, because a longer run brings a bigger reward.
