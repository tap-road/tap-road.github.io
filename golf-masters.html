Home › Golf Masters

Golf Masters is a 3D mini golf game with 174 holes across seven themed worlds. You travel from Timber Woods and Frosty Hills to Temple Jungle and Magma Isle. Each world brings new obstacles like sand traps, ice, geysers, moving platforms and lava. Your task is to sink the ball in as few strokes as you can. In Battles, up to six players race through three holes to win cups!

How to Play Golf Masters Unblocked?

  • Shooting: Drag back from the ball to set power, move sideways to aim, then let go.
  • Keyboard: Aim with A/D or the Left/Right arrow keys. Set power with W/S or Up/Down, then press Spacebar to shoot.
  • Camera: Press C for free look, or drag anywhere else to turn the view.
  • Upgrades: Spend coins on club upgrades for power, accuracy, guideline and luck.
