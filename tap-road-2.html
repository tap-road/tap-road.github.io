Tap Road 2 is the sequel to Tap Road, and it keeps the same endless ball runner idea. You guide a glowing sphere down a neon road and try to beat your own best distance. This time the track brings four new obstacle types. Challenge Mode adds six special runs, such as Double Lane, where you handle two spheres at once. The longer you last, the faster and trickier it gets!

How to Play Tap Road 2 Unblocked?

  • Picking a mode: Start a normal endless run, or open Challenge Mode and choose a challenge.
  • Steering: Keep the sphere away from every obstacle, since one mistake ends the run.
  • Gravity Shift: In this challenge, hold to lift the sphere against gravity.
  • Gems: Collect them when it is safe to unlock new spheres.
