Home › Speed Stars

Speed Stars is a track and field running game built on rhythm. You race in sprints, hurdles and relays. How fast your runner goes depends on how well you time each step. Mashing the keys won't win. A smooth and steady rhythm will. Longer events like the 400m and 800m also test how well you save stamina for the final stretch!

How to Play Speed Stars Unblocked?

  • Running: Press the Left and Right arrow keys one after the other.
  • Starting: Go right when the starting pistol fires, but never before it.
  • Hurdles: Choose 100m Hurdles or 400m Hurdles. Keep the Down arrow key held as each barrier comes up.
  • Relay: In the 4x100m Relay, tap the Down arrow key as you reach your teammate to hand off the baton.
  • Customizing: Choose any athlete except the default one and change their look. Some items cost gems.
