Escape Road City 2 is the next chapter in the Escape Road series. You are a getaway driver, and the police chase starts right away. The city has streets, rivers, rooftops and highways to race through. This time you can leave your car to run, swim or take another vehicle. You can even launch into the air and glide away with a parachute!

How to Play Escape Road City 2 Unblocked?

  • Driving: Use the arrow keys or WASD to move.
  • Changing vehicles: Press Space to get out of a car or into a new one.
  • Power-ups: Press F or tap the on-screen button to use them.
  • Water: Swimming is fine, but staying under too long ends the run.
