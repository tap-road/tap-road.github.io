Home › Wacky Flip

Wacky Flip is a skill game that mixes parkour, gymnastics and ragdoll physics. You play a backflip athlete doing stunts around town. The game has 8 levels, from The Court and Frontyard to The Playground 2, and each level holds 10 flip challenges. Clean landings in a row raise your multiplier and your score. Along the way, you unlock new moves like the Pike, Scissor and Knee Tuck!

How to Play Wacky Flip Unblocked?

  • Crouching: Click or touch and hold to crouch and time your jump.
  • Jumping: Let go to take off.
  • Flipping: Hold again in the air to spin, and keep holding to finish the rotation.
  • Landing: Release to get ready to land, then aim for a clean finish on the platform.
